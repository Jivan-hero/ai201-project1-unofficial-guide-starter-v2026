THREAD: Do transfer credits actually count toward the major?

--- reply 1 (20 votes) ---
Toward general requirements almost always. Toward the major it's case-by-case and the department decides, not the registrar.

--- reply 2 (25 votes) ---
Get it in writing from the department before you rely on it. I had a verbal yes that didn't survive a staff change.

--- reply 3 (13 votes) ---
The credit hours count for registration priority and the housing lottery even when they don't count toward the major, which is worth knowing.

ENGL 205 Writing for the Sciences

Transferred in last year, so take this with a grain of salt. Format is workshop format, you write every week and peers read it. Assessment: no exams; a portfolio of six revised pieces. Not curved.

Expect 4 to 5 hours a week, mostly writing and rewriting.

The one piece of advice: the portfolio is graded on revision, so keep your drafts — you're marked on the distance travelled.
